**Google Search Console adds dedicated AI reporting**

Google has expanded Search Console with dedicated reporting for generative search.

Website owners can now separately view data on how their pages appear in AI Overviews, AI Mode and other generative elements of Google Search.

Testing began in June 2026 with a limited number of websites. The global rollout began on August 31, 2026.

The reports provide data on impressions, pages, countries, devices and dates. This makes it possible to track a website’s visibility in AI search separately, without blending it entirely with conventional search statistics.

For SEO, this is an important change: AI visibility on Google can now be analyzed as a distinct source of search presence.

Source: [Google Search Console](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports), August 31, 2026.
