## Which SEO services does SEOImpuls provide?

The current service scope includes [technical SEO audits](/seo/), [keyword research and search-intent mapping](/services/keyword-research/) and [one-to-one SEO consultation](/services/seo-consultation/). The [services directory](/seo-services/) explains what each type of work is intended to deliver.

## What information is needed to start?

Provide the website URL, target country and language, products or services, the main search visibility problem and the pages that matter most. If you have existing Search Console or analytics reports, they can help establish a baseline. Do not send passwords through chat.

## Do you require access to Google Search Console?

A preliminary public-site assessment can start without private access. More detailed analysis of search performance, indexing trends and query-to-page relationships is stronger with relevant Search Console data. Access requirements must be defined before work begins.

## What does a technical SEO audit deliver?

The scope may include crawling and indexability findings, HTTP status and redirect reviews, canonical and sitemap checks, page-template issues, internal linking, important on-page problems and a prioritized list of recommended changes. The exact pages, report format and implementation responsibility are agreed for each project.

## How does keyword research differ from an SEO audit?

Keyword research focuses on search demand: how people phrase needs, which intents belong together and which pages should answer them. A technical audit concentrates on the ability of the website to be discovered, rendered, indexed and navigated. They complement each other but are not interchangeable.

## Do you create a separate page for every keyword?

No. Different keywords can express the same search intent. A useful page should answer a coherent user need; keyword-to-URL mapping also helps avoid competing pages with overlapping purposes.

## How quickly will search results improve?

There is no universal timetable or guaranteed increase in rankings. Some technical corrections can be verified immediately on the website, while Google's crawling, indexing and search visibility may take longer and depend on factors outside the site's control. We distinguish completed implementation from changes in organic performance.

## Are page changes or implementation included?

Only if implementation is explicitly included in the agreed service scope. An audit or consultation can provide guidance without editing your website. Larger migrations, design work, content production and ongoing monitoring require separate specifications.

## Can you work on multilingual websites?

A technical review can include language URLs, canonical signals and hreflang relationships. Each language also needs a review of its own search terms and content intent. Multilingual scope and any required language expertise must be agreed before work starts.

## Should I remove or redirect my existing service pages?

Not without reviewing their search visibility, incoming external links, purpose and suitable replacement pages. A permanent redirect should point to a genuinely relevant destination. Where no equivalent content exists, keeping a misleading redirect can be worse than retiring the URL correctly.

## How is progress measured?

Baseline measurements can include important indexable URLs, technical issues, Search Console impressions and clicks, relevant landing-page queries and qualified enquiries. Outcomes should be interpreted against the agreed goals. Third-party authority scores alone are not evidence of Google ranking improvement.

## How are pricing and project scope determined?

The scope depends on website size, available data, markets, languages and implementation requirements. Individual service pages may display reference prices; final deliverables, current pricing and timing must be confirmed before payment.
