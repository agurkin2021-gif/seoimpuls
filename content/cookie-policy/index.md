## 1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser to support website functionality, security, and analytics.

## 2. Cookie Categories We Use

### Essential cookies

Required for core site operation and consent-state persistence.  
These cookies are always active and cannot be disabled from the consent panel.

### Analytics cookies

Used only after your explicit consent to analytics storage (Google Analytics).

## 3. Cookie Details

| Cookie | Provider | Category | Purpose | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `seoimpuls_cookie_consent_v2` | SeoImpuls | Essential | Stores your cookie consent choice (including timestamp/version metadata) | 365 days |
| `_ga` | Google | Analytics | Distinguishes users for analytics | up to 2 years |
| `_ga_*` | Google | Analytics | Persists session/state for GA4 | up to 2 years |
| `_gid` | Google | Analytics | Distinguishes users over short periods | up to 24 hours |
| `_gat` (if used) | Google | Analytics | Request-rate management | about 1 minute |

Cookie names and durations can change if Google updates its services or configuration.

## 4. When Cookies Are Set

- essential cookies may be set when you use the site
- analytics cookies are set only after you provide consent

## 5. Legal Basis

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies:

- essential cookies: legitimate interest / strictly necessary operation
- analytics cookies: consent

## 6. How to Manage Consent

You can change your choice anytime via **Cookie Settings** in the footer.

You can also manage cookies in browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.).

## 7. Browser Opt-Out Signals

Where required by applicable U.S. state law, we treat recognized browser opt-out preference signals (for example, Global Privacy Control) as requests to disable non-essential analytics.

## 8. Third Parties

When analytics is enabled by consent, data may be processed by Google according to Google’s terms and policies.

## 9. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes.  
Please check the “Last updated” date.
