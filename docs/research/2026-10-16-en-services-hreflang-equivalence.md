## Summary of translation intent

- All three service catalogues share `translationKey: services` and navigate among real SEO offerings. Technical URL relationships are generated by `layouts/partials/head/link.html`, from `.AllTranslations` plus English `x-default`.
- The EN catalogue contains **three prominent entry paths plus 23 white-hat workstreams in four detailed groups**. RU and ES currently each contain **four prominently linked offers**, including website development, but do **not** carry equivalent 23-workstream editorial coverage or the sample evidence report.
- Thus they have the same broad user task — choose an SEO-related service — but their **content parity is partial, not a verified full translation**. Reciprocity checks can pass while this substantive difference remains. Do not claim perfect hreflang localization readiness.
- There is no evidence these mismatches incur an automatic penalty. The practical risks are a user arriving in another language and not finding the same information, market-intent mismatch and potentially inconsistent service scope expectations.

## Actual hreflang mechanics and limitations

- `hugo.toml` languageCode: EN `en-US`, RU `ru-RU`, ES `es-ES`; template lowercases to `en-us`, `ru-ru`, `es-es`. An English `x-default` points to the EN version.
- `scripts/verify_en_services_route.py` verifies reciprocal English ↔ RU/ES URLs in generated HTML. The schema and sitemap tests had passed previously. This proves URL mechanics, not that every workstream has corresponding translated copy.
- Spanish copy contains voseo/Latin American expressions (e.g., `Conocé`), whereas `es-ES` specifies Spain. The business's precise Spanish target region is **not established** in this source. **Do not switch automatically to `es-AR` or generic `es`** without confirmed market decision.
- The GitHub Pages preview deploy process injects `noindex,nofollow`; preview should not be an indexed alternate. Live production `seoimpuls.com` still requires coordinated deployment, real HTTP responses and user-approved migration rules. Existing production locale pages do not validate new preview content.
- Different offers are not inherently spam or a canonical duplication issue, but truly interchangeable language alternatives should answer equivalent tasks. Here broad task matches, detailed content coverage differs.
