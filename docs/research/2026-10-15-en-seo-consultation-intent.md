## Legacy SEOImpuls problems
- SEO consultation was buried under the link-building product namespace with generic H1 and large marketing text.
- Included "Link building and its role in SEO" and unsupported claims of completed cases.
- Claimed around 3 hours, $350 and indefinite ongoing long-term support without evidence that unlimited follow-up was deliverable.
- BuilderJson included absolute pixel offsets and Times New Roman overrides likely to hurt responsive behavior.

## Improvements
- Distinct H1/Title "SEO Consultation & Strategy Review", concise description, clean URL.
- Practical suitability, topics, preparation, process, scope, limits, outcome, FAQ, contact.
- Previous price $350 and approx three-hour session presented as *prior published reference terms to confirm*, not an unconditional promise.
- No assertions of unlimited support, guaranteed ranks, or unverified case studies.
- Reuse existing Hugo builders without fragile absolute-position offsets; add third real service to /services/ directory.
- Preserve original URL as candidate for later relevant 301; no redirect configured now.
