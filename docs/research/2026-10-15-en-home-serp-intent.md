The ten URLs below are sampled non-advertising organic-looking competitor/reference pages. Confirm target geography and actual GSC query performance before finalizing positioning.

## Problems found in baseline
Homepage H1 is "SEO Backlink Service"; core copy revolves around backlinks/behavioural factor speculation and an unrelated VPS affiliate promotion. EN menu links promote link building and software rather than legitimate SEO. Automatically injected featured services can resurrect prohibited content; fake sample news text is hard-coded as fallback.

## Corrections in this change
Changed EN H1/title/description, relevant home service cards, deliverable-based explanatory section, three-step working process, legitimate CTA and EN navigation. Removed irrelevant home-page VPS promo and exposed backlink-facing blocks from EN rendering. Kept site layout and design scaffold.
