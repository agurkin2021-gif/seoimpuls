# FAQ SEOImpuls — commercial support and trust (2026-10-15)

## User tasks / intent
The FAQ primarily supports brand and service selection, not an independent high-volume search landing page. Avoid competing with /seo/, /services/keyword-research/ or /services/seo-consultation/ in title and long-form content. Give direct, concise factual answers and link to specific services where details belong.
