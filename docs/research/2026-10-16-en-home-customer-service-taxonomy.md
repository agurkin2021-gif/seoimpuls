# English homepage — customer-facing SEO service mix, 2026-10-16

## Owner decision
The three current service detail URLs are only detailed entry points; the buyer-facing EN homepage must show the wider range of actual SEO work without making nine new indexed pages. Nine groups were chosen for a legible three-column desktop / two-column tablet / single-column mobile grid. Each name uses a recognizable English SEO service phrase while its supporting text explains a concrete buyer problem and an example of the scoped result. Detailed implementation is never automatic.

## Nine areas and intent partition
1. **SEO Audit** — Find out what is holding your existing website back in Google search. Typical result: Website audit report with problems and suggested priorities. Existing details: `/technical-seo-audit/`.
2. **Keyword Research** — Find the searches customers use for your products or services, even before you have a website. Typical result: Keyword research spreadsheet grouped by topic. Existing details: `/keyword-research/`.
3. **On-Page SEO** — Review page titles, headings, wording and links so visitors can understand what each page offers. Typical result: Specific recommendations for improving existing pages. Link to the homepage's existing project-enquiry section; quote/coverage must be agreed.
4. **Technical SEO** — Check why important pages may not appear in Google and find issues with speed, redirects or page access. Typical result: Technical problem list with recommended checks and fixes. Link to the homepage's existing project-enquiry section; quote/coverage must be agreed.
5. **SEO Competitor Analysis** — See which competitor pages appear in Google and what useful topics your business may be missing. Typical result: Competitor comparison and missed-search opportunities. Link to the homepage's existing project-enquiry section; quote/coverage must be agreed.
6. **SEO Content Optimization** — Review existing website text and plan content that answers the questions your customers search for. Typical result: Page and content improvement priorities. Link to the homepage's existing project-enquiry section; quote/coverage must be agreed.
7. **Local SEO** — Check how customers can find your business in local Google searches and on Maps, where relevant. Typical result: Local search visibility review and recommendations. Link to the homepage's existing project-enquiry section; quote/coverage must be agreed.
8. **International SEO** — Check whether people in each target country can find the right language version of your website. Typical result: Review of language pages and country-specific search issues. Link to the homepage's existing project-enquiry section; quote/coverage must be agreed.
9. **SEO Consultation** — Discuss your business goals, ask SEO questions and decide which work is worth doing first. Typical result: Practical guidance; a written plan is quoted separately. Existing details: `/seo-consultation/`.

### Intent boundary
- Homepage / remains broad Professional SEO Services: what SEO work can help a business, and how to choose.
- /technical-seo-audit/ is an existing report-focused detail page (do not pretend it guarantees implementation); /keyword-research/ is distinct research deliverable; /seo-consultation/ is advice, written plan separate unless agreed.
- On-page/content/competitor/technical/local/international variants on home are short categories, not new doorway URLs. For the six without approved dedicated route, enquiry is explicit.
- SEO Audit = broad diagnosis/priorities; Technical SEO = a narrower check of site access, indexing, speed/redirects. Avoid duplicate landing pages or an implied two-package upsell.
- On-Page SEO = improving existing page elements; SEO Content Optimization = research/written recommendations about text/pages, not promised copywriting. Local/International limited to relevant sites/markets.
- No fabricated case studies, people, results, prices, search volume or guarantee.
- RU/ES sources and EN service detail content unchanged.
