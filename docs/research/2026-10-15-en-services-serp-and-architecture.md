## Proposed EN catalog improvements (no fictitious products)
- Header/H1 should emphasize **Choose an SEO Service**, not repeat the homepage's `Professional SEO Services`. Title should still mention SEO Services and a practical deliverable choice, not claim "20 types" or "packages". Preserve clean intentional hierarchy.
- Distinct first-screen answer: what is available and how the buyer selects between audit / keyword mapping / consultation; use three existing audited service URLs, no manufactured fourth offer.
- One concise buyer comparison (problem, service, usual deliverable, implementation responsibilities) *may* help the "compare options" intent, but avoid duplicating homepage's cards or another technical-case example.
- Keep costs conditional on actual site/scope. No false SLA, no invented fixed pricing, unverifiable rankings, physical offices, case studies or broad agency/team size.
- No extra blog-like keyword stuffing to target article SERPs. No fake FAQPage/rating schema. Watch current Hugo shared partial: generic `BlogPosting` on commercial content pages needs a separate template-level audit, but preserve other pages until separately authorized.
- Keep original `/services/keyword-research/` and `/services/seo-consultation/` URLs in this first pass. Moving child URLs would be a second, higher-risk operation requiring GSC/migration review.

## Changing /services/ safely — change control
- Google recommends descriptive URLs and hyphens (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/url-structure), but changing URL **is a migration**, not an isolated keyword insertion (https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/site-move-with-url-changes).
- If approved, make `/seo-services/` the **only full indexed catalog page**, self canonical in production. Do not publish duplicate content at `/services/`.
- Hugo alias at old `/services/` can provide a **static preview fallback**, but is **not HTTP 301**. For production if relevant, configure **server 301** `/services/` → `/seo-services/` only when deploying and after GSC/migration review.
- Update EN menu and link dependencies from homepage, About, FAQ; preserve actual child routes and RU/ES content. Hreflang references of the translated service-hub counterparts should point to new English canonical after build, not stale URL.
- Validate single canonical, schema, sitemap contains only `/seo-services/`, anchors and website links remain 200, mobile/desktop browser, Hugo strict gate and Pages Preview. CI must be updated for new path and must distinguish alias from actual page.
- **No production changes in this stage; no claim search advantage or release approval.**
