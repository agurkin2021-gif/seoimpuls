# About SEOImpuls — company trust and scope audit (EN, 2026-10-15)

The About URL is primarily a brand/navigation and commercial trust page, not a target for artificial generic SEO query rankings. A Google top-ten benchmark for generic SEO keywords is not an appropriate content standard for an organization About page.

## Baseline problems
- Description presented the business as SEO strategy plus link services.
- Claimed years of practical link-building experience and large Kwork review/order totals (2,900+/1,690+/5.0 and 100%).
- The previous claims were not independently validated from current authenticated platform data. Older user reviews may concern materially different legacy services; they must not imply that new white-hat SEO services produced them.
- Page lacked scope, methodology, acceptance criteria and concrete service responsibilities.

## Corrections
- New H1 "About SEOImpuls" with unique description and substantive methodology.
- Service categories: technical SEO, search intent and page maps, content/on-page, consultation.
- Transparent evidence and scope policy; no guarantee of placements, invented cases, named clients or unverified current statistics.
- Includes contact methods and appropriate internal links. Existing design builder, URL, locale translationKey and page identity retained.
