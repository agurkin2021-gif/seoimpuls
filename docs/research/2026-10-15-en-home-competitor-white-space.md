## Priority-ranked whitespace options for SEOImpuls EN homepage

### Priority 1 — demonstrate an SEO finding (most valuable)
Add **one compact, accessible specimen** below existing `What You Receive`, not another generic 8-part SEO sales list. Label exactly "Illustrative SEO finding — not a client case". Fields:
- **Issue / affected pages:** Intended-to-rank page has contradictory indexing signals (generic illustrative scenario, no invented client URL).
- **Evidence:** HTTP response, rendered robots directive, canonical and relevant Search Console inspection when access is granted.
- **Proposed fix / owner:** Confirm intended indexing; a named responsible role approves and implements the selected correction (varies per project).
- **Done when:** HTTP/robots/canonical configuration rechecked against accepted intent, with result recorded. **This verifies the implementation, not Google indexing or rankings.**

A displayed *format* is a real preview of what a useful recommendation contains, but not an actual delivered case study. It answers the Reddit buyer concern "what is done and how will I know?".

### Priority 2 — explicit client/provider handoff
Add 1–2 sentences to the same area or existing `Define SEO Scope` explaining **who diagnoses, who approves, who implements and who validates** as mutually agreed roles. Do not imply SEOImpuls edits the client's site by default. The current homepage already says "roles agreed" but does not demonstrate one complete issue handoff.

### Priority 3 — least-privilege access reassurance
Within bottom `Discuss Your Website's SEO Priorities` section, a small line: "A public-site review can begin without account access; if Search Console data is needed for a deeper audit, read-only access is sufficient initially. Do not send passwords by chat." This is supported by existing `/faq/` and Google Search Central guidance. It is a purchase friction reducer, not a keyword-targeting gimmick.

### Priority 4 — URL-level semantic decisions (micro specimen)
A small example of grouping two synonymous queries onto one strong URL and separating distinct commercial intent into another URL; **illustrative only**. Could be more appropriate for supporting `/services/keyword-research/` **later** under the existing EN-only-homepage scope. Avoid duplicating keyword service content on the general homepage.

### Priority 5 — eligibility / limitations
A simple specialist-written "When we would first fix site access or implement approved changes before suggesting more content" decision rule. Only publish it if SEOImpuls commits operationally; otherwise it is an unsupported promise. Do not invent anti-spam compliance statements without actual practice matching them.

## Do NOT add as presumed gaps
- Standard "what is SEO", SEO benefits, H1 keyword variants or extra H2 sections duplicating the current eight service cards and commercial FAQs: those are **already covered**.
- Generic "reporting" / "transparent pricing" / "no guaranteed rankings": several competitors already explain these; not unique.
- Unlimited SEO, AI citations guarantees, paid link manipulations, backlink packages, 500 case studies, a named team, years of experience, badges or exact project price without real verifiable evidence.
- More than one new prominent section on the homepage without user-first intent justification; lengthy SEO copy can worsen scanning and conversion.
- New service URLs or edits of RU/ES and supporting EN pages: current owner scope is EN homepage only.
